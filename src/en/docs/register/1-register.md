## Open the registration page

1. Open the registration page and click **Register** in the top-right corner.
2. From the login page, click **Don't have an account? Register** at the bottom.

## Option 1 (recommended): Sign up with Google

1. Click **Continue with Google**.
2. Select the Google account you want to link and complete authorization.
3. Your PackyAPI account will be created and signed in automatically.

No separate password is required when you use Google. Select the same Google account the next time you sign in.

## Option 2: Sign up with email

1. Click **Sign up with username**.
2. Enter your email address, username, and password.
3. Submit the form and follow the on-screen instructions.

::: warning Keep your credentials safe
Your email may be used for verification and service notifications. Use a strong password with letters, numbers, and special characters.
:::
