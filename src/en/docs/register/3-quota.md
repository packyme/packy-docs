After signing in, open **Wallet Management** in the left menu.

## Add quota

1. Select a preset amount under **Select quota**, or enter an amount under **Custom quota**.
2. Confirm the **Amount due** and click **Pay now**.

::: info Payment notes
The current rate is `1:1`: one Chinese yuan equals one US-dollar quota. If Alipay or WeChat Pay does not open the payment page, disable your proxy and try again.
:::

::: warning Invoice policy
Invoice availability depends on the payment entity and order date. Personal payments currently support overseas invoices; mainland invoices are available only for eligible corporate payments.
:::
