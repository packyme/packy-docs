## How to View the Latest Groups

1. In the console dashboard, click "Model Marketplace" in the top right corner to view groups and models.

2. In the Model Marketplace, the left panel displays the **Token Groups** referenced in the [Create API Token](/en/docs/register/4-token.html) step, while the right panel shows all models available under that group.

3. The multiplier shown next to the group name (e.g., `x0.5`) indicates the pricing rate:
    - Different token groups offer different billing rates.
    - Rate < 1 indicates a discount: `0.8x` = 20% off, `0.5x` = 50% off.
    - Rate > 1 indicates premium pricing: `1.5x` = 50% additional charge.
    - Standard default rate is `1x`.

::: warning Why This Step Matters
Understanding token groups ensures you know exactly which models are supported by each group. Choosing the wrong group will lead to "Model does not exist" or routing errors.

**We provide this detailed guide to help you choose the right group for your specific tool and use case.**
:::
