## Overview

Our Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to any person or entity submitting input to PackyMe's products and/or services, including through authorized resellers or pass-through access ("Users"). The policy is designed to maintain safety, security, and responsible application of our AI systems.

PackyMe employs detection, moderation, and monitoring mechanisms. If a user violates this policy, PackyMe may restrict, suspend, or terminate access, and may block, filter, or modify violating inputs or outputs.

::: tip Reading Recommendations
- If you deploy user-facing applications, review the "High-Risk Use Case Requirements" and "Additional Use Case Guidelines".
- If you are unsure whether a specific use case is permitted, review the "Universal Usage Standards".
:::

## Universal Usage Standards

### 1. No Illegal Activities or Violations of Law
Do not use our services to engage in, facilitate, or promote illegal activities, including illicit drug transactions, human trafficking, prostitution, intellectual property violations, child abuse, or other unlawful activities.

### 2. No Harm to Critical Infrastructure
Do not use our services to disrupt, impair, or compromise critical infrastructure, including telecommunications, healthcare, energy, transportation, water utilities, financial clearing, election systems, or military facilities.

### 3. No Cyberattacks or Network Compromise
Do not develop, deploy, automate, or assist in vulnerability exploitation, social engineering, malware, ransomware, denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, botnets, unauthorized interception, or circumvention of security controls.

### 4. No Weapons Development
Do not use our services to design, produce, test, market, or procure weapons, explosives, CBRN materials, military-grade cyber weapons, missiles, combat drones, or autonomous lethal weapon systems.

### 5. No Incitement of Violence or Hate
Do not incite, promote, or glorify extremist violence, identity-based violence, harassment, discrimination, or hate speech targeting protected groups.

### 6. No Violations of Privacy or Identity Rights
Do not engage in stalking, harassment, or unauthorized surveillance. Do not build facial recognition systems in private/restricted areas or harvest personal, health, biometric, or confidential data without consent.

### 7. Child Safety & Zero Exploitation
Strictly prohibited: Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, sexual extortion, advice on concealing child abuse, sexual roleplay involving minors, or content glorifying child exploitation.

### 8. No Psychologically or Emotionally Harmful Content
Do not generate or distribute content promoting self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or targeted bullying/harassment.

### 9. No Misinformation or Deceptive Campaigns
Do not generate or disseminate mass deceptive content on public health emergencies, civic processes, laws, regulations, or democratic institutions.

### 10. No Interference with Democratic Processes
Do not use services for voter suppression, covert automated messaging to public officials, election interference, political manipulation campaigns, or deceptive political deepfakes.

### 11. No Criminal Justice, Predictive Policing, or Prohibited Enforcement
Do not use services for predictive policing, judicial sentencing, bail/parole decisions, lie detection, emotional recognition, social credit scoring, or bulk surveillance.

### 12. No Fraudulent, Predatory, or Deceptive Practices
Do not generate scams, phishing schemes, forged documents, fake reviews, pyramid schemes, payday loan exploitation, predatory debt collection, or spam.

### 13. Platform Integrity & Reverse Engineering
Do not attempt to extract model weights, system prompts, hidden instructions, training data, or response patterns via unauthorized scraping, jailbreaks, prompt injection, distillation, or rate-limit evasion.

### 14. No Sexually Explicit Content
Do not generate pornographic or sexually explicit material. Exceptions apply to legitimate scientific, artistic, educational, or reproductive healthcare use cases.

## High-Risk Use Cases

For domains that directly impact personal well-being, rights, and critical life opportunities, the following mandatory safeguards apply:

::: important Mandatory Safeguards
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** When providing advice or decisions that directly impact individuals or consumers, a qualified professional in the field must review the content or decision prior to finalization and dissemination.
- **AI Disclosure:** You must clearly disclose to end users that AI is involved in generating the recommendation, decision, or output at the beginning of each session.
:::

High-risk use cases include:

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Legal** | Statutory interpretation, legal guidance, and legal drafting |
| **Healthcare** | Medical diagnosis, clinical decisions, patient treatment, mental health guidance |
| **Insurance** | Underwriting decisions, claim processing, coverage determination |
| **Finance** | Investment advice, creditworthiness scoring, loan approval decisions |
| **Employment & Housing** | Resume screening, hiring decisions, performance evaluations, tenant screening, mortgage eligibility |
| **Academic Testing & Admissions** | Standardized testing evaluation, grading, university admissions, institutional accreditation |
| **Media & Journalism** | Automated content generation published directly for mass public consumption |

## Additional Use Case Guidelines

- **Chatbots & Conversational Agents**: Must disclose to users that they are interacting with an AI at the start of every session.
- **Products for Minors**: Must follow child safety guidelines.
- **Autonomous Agent Workflows**: Must adhere to all AUP standards within tool-calling environments.
- **MCP Servers**: Model Context Protocol servers listed in directories must adhere to connector policies.
